§ 06 — QUESTIONS

Good questions.

How long does it take?

Every package starts from 7 days. Bigger builds take longer, and the clock starts once we have your deposit and your content: photos, services and prices. You’ll get firm dates in your written proposal.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and once the balance is paid, the website, its content and your brand files are yours. At hand-over you get the logins to everything.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work, your services and prices, and how you like clients to book and pay. After our first call we send you a short checklist, so nothing gets missed.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Plenty of people start with Squire and move up once bookings pick up. What you’ve already paid is deducted from the bigger package, and we build on what you already have rather than starting again.

What if I already have a logo?

Good, we’ll work with it. Every package includes a clean-up of your existing logo, colours and type. If it can’t be saved, a new logo is an add-on, and Crown includes a full brand identity.

What happens if I stop the retainer?

Give us 30 days’ notice and that’s it. Your site keeps running and stays yours. We make sure you have every login, and from then on renewals and updates are in your hands. You can always come back for once-off help.

Not sure which one fits?

Book a free 30-minute call. We’ll look at how you work now and tell you honestly which package makes sense, even if it’s the smallest one.
