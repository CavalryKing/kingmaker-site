LightVille Games

A South African game studio and the online community that has grown up around it.

Visit lightvillegames.com ↗
THE CHALLENGE

The studio, its games and its community all needed to feel like one brand, with a proper home online and the systems to keep it organised as it grew.

WHAT WE BUILT
  • Brand architecture for the studio and its products
  • The lightvillegames.com website
  • Interactive handbooks for the community, each on its own subdomain
  • The systems that run behind all of it
THE RESULT

A mod blog that had lost its direction became LightVille Games: one brand from studio to server, with the rules, handbooks, legal pages and game downloads players need, all in one place on lightvillegames.com.

JN Luxe

Natural nail health, done as house calls across Cape Town, with its own nail serum, BONURI.

Visit jnluxe.com ↗
THE CHALLENGE

Every booking and every serum order came in through WhatsApp. Dates, addresses and payments lived in chat threads, and each new client meant the same back-and-forth before anything was confirmed.

WHAT WE BUILT
  • A brand direction for the business
  • A new jnluxe.com with shop and booking pages
  • In progress: automated booking, deposits and checkout
THE RESULT

Bookings went from WhatsApp-only to 90% through the new site, so clients now pick a time and book on their own instead of messaging back and forth.
